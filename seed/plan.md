## 1. Executive summary

ZMANI (zmani.ai, Tbilisi) lets men start from a character or celebrity whose style they like, such as Tommy Shelby or James Bond, and get the complete look: every item, its price and a link to buy it. An AI try-on shows the look on the user's own photo, and the user unticks the items already owned and buys only the rest. Today the site is live in Georgian and English with 11 characters and 24 complete looks (as of 16 November 2026), built by a team of 3. No user, revenue or sales figures are reported yet and no partner is signed; talks with LC Waikiki are ongoing without an agreement. We are raising $1.5M pre-seed at $10M pre-money ($11.5M post) to prove demand and unit economics in Georgia first, then expand.

**Team.** Nika Abashidze, Founder & CEO. Ran caru.ge, a Georgian car marketplace, ~8 years. 500 Startups; Forbes Georgia 30 Under 30. Levani, Growth & paid ads. Growth and paid ads. Giorgi, Operations. Operations.

## 2. Problem

Men often like a specific style, a character's or a celebrity's, but rebuilding it means finding every item one by one, matching them, and guessing how the whole look will sit on them. Most give up or buy pieces that don't work together. This is ZMANI's core hypothesis; its scale and commercial value still have to be proven with user data, which is the first job of this round.

What the competitors' own reviews say (seed/competitors.json, 2026-11-24):

- shoppin: 3 of 22 recent 1 and 2 star reviews mention bugs and app quality.
- Gensmo: 5 of 25 recent 1 and 2 star reviews mention bugs and app quality.
- Dressly: 68 of 226 recent 1 and 2 star reviews mention payments and refunds.
- Alta Daily: 5 of 28 recent 1 and 2 star reviews mention bugs and app quality.

## 3. Solution

ZMANI makes the complete look the unit of shopping. The user picks a character, opens that character's complete looks, sees each item with its price and store link, tries the look on their own photo with AI (about 20 seconds per the product description; no full speed and quality benchmark yet), unticks what they already own and buys the rest through the stores' links. ZMANI does not run its own checkout, warehouse or delivery today, and makes no claim about AI sizing or fewer returns.

- Complete looks: every item, price, link
- AI try-on on your own photo
- Buy only what you're missing

## 4. Market

We size the launch market from official data. Geostat's 2024 census counts 1,881,004 men in Georgia. Clothing spend per person is our assumption, built from Geostat's 2025 household figures (603 GEL per household member a month, clothing 5.8%), about $160 a year. That gives a Georgian men's clothing TAM of about $301M, a Tbilisi SAM of about $98M (612,483 men) and a SOM of 2% of SAM in 3 years, about $1.96M. Armenia, Azerbaijan and Turkey are planned next and are not counted here.

## 6. Business model

Two planned stages. 2027: complete looks and all item links are free; the only paid feature is AI try-on at a planned GEL 0.50 a photo, sold as credit top-ups so each photo is not a separate card charge. After 18% VAT that is GEL 0.42 of revenue; with planning estimates of GEL 0.16 AI compute, GEL 0.01 payments and GEL 0.01 support, the contribution is about GEL 0.24 a photo before acquisition and fixed costs. From late 2027 ZMANI plans to sell looks itself, starting as a limited pilot, with a working assumption of supplier cost at about 70% of retail (about 30% gross margin, not net profit); no supplier has agreed to these terms yet.
